Our Story

From one spreadsheet to a full success team.

2018

One spreadsheet, one care coordinator

Marcus started care management out of a shared spreadsheet, tracking appointments by hand for eleven patients he called every week.

2020

Remote care management, same standard

We took the weekly check-in model online — the same close care management, minus the requirement to be in the same city.

2022

Reporting lab opens

Added attendance modeling and structured planning, so "patient-flow health" became a number we could actually show you, not a feeling.

2025

640+ patients, nine care coordinators

Still capped patient-per-coordinator ratios on purpose. We'd rather turn people away than let care management quality slide.

Why We Cap Ratios

We turn people away on purpose.

Every care coordinator at Stackly caps how many patients they'll take on. It means we say no more often than we'd like — and it means the patients we do take on get seen, every single week.

No care coordinator carries more patients than they can actually watch closely A waitlist exists so quality doesn't slide when demand spikes Every check-in gets a real reply — never a queue of unread messages

What We Believe

Three rules we don't bend on.

Systems beat motivation

A good care routine keeps moving even on the weeks you don't feel like it. We build the process so willpower isn't the thing holding your growth together.

Care management is weekly, not quarterly

A dashboard that flags your numbers isn't a care coordinator. Every patient gets a real human checking their care plan and their week, every single week.

Honest numbers over hype

We measure, we re-measure, and we tell you the real number — even when it's not the one you wanted to hear that month.
